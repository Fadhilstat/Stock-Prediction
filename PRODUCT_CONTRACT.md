## 3. Core Product Mission
Ruang Risiko IDX is a serious Indonesian equity research, risk, probabilistic forecasting, and pre-buy decision-support platform.
Key capabilities include:
- Validated IDX market data pipeline with strict quarantine for corrupt or unverified rows
- Interactive time-series charts with genuine analytical capability
- Linked Day Inspector synchronizing all point-in-time evidence available at a given trading cutoff
- Multi-horizon probabilistic forecasting (1D, 5D, 20D) with explicit uncertainty quantiles (q10, q25, q50, q75, q90)
- Forecast calibration evaluation (Brier score, log loss, reliability curves, interval coverage)
- Scenario engine (bull, base, bear, volatility shock, regime reversal)
- Deterministic technical research indicators and ICT-style research hypotheses
- Point-in-time fundamental intelligence, earnings quality, and valuation regimes
- Corporate actions and disclosures impact laboratory
- IHSG macroeconomic and market context, sector intelligence, and market breadth
- Liquidity research, execution slippage proxies, and exit capacity analysis
- Point-in-time foreign and broker flow evidence where reliable
- Indonesian equity creator and social intelligence with lead-lag and crowding detection
- Multimodal fusion with mandatory ablation benchmarks and evidence conflict radar
- Strategy governance with realistic Indonesian transaction cost assumptions
- True forward testing with immutable Prediction Journal and Prediction Replay
- Risk Engine with final veto authority over all directional signals
- Structured Pre-Buy Decision Passport artifact before any purchase consideration

## 4. Finish Product First Policy
Before release candidate freezing, the product candidate must be:
- Functionally complete for defined scope
- Locally validated and production-build capable
- Usable on desktop (1440px), laptop (1024px), tablet (768px), and mobile (360px, 412px)
- Usable via mouse, touch (minimum 44px targets), and keyboard navigation
- Data validated, point-in-time safe, and lookahead free
- Fully audited under Anti-Slop principles with no decorative or misleading elements

## 5. Absolute No Em Dash Rule
Never use an em dash (Unicode U+2014) anywhere in:
- Source code
- Comments and docstrings
- UI copy and labels
- Documentation and markdown files
- Configuration, commit messages, and reports
Rewrite sentences naturally using hyphens, colons, parentheses, or separate sentences.
